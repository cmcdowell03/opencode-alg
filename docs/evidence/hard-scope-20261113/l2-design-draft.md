# L2 retention protocol — development draft for review

Design only. This is a prospective design, not evidence that its study has run. Existing user authorization governs implementation; this draft alone does not issue a paid cohort. V1 only; extend the existing benchmark lifecycle and evidence files. Do not reinterpret L1 results or change historical controls.

## Decision and current evidence boundary

Measure two different things: how often compaction actually happens during useful work, and whether independently verified task obligations cease to govern behavior after a particular transition. A marker, a retained file, or a checker saying “passed” does not answer the second question. L1 with no scored compaction exposure has an **unavailable information-loss rate**, not zero loss. A complete census with no compactions can support an observed compaction frequency of zero over its stated duration/turns; it cannot support a retention claim.

Native host pruning is enabled too. A complete compaction-marker census is not necessarily a complete census of context changes: pruning can remove retained tool/context material without a completed-summary marker. Where pruning is unobservable, zero compaction markers cannot establish unchanged context, zero pruning, or no information loss.

## Proposed bounded development design

Use two fresh, separately seeded incidents: (A) pending remote work/result reuse after an accepted operation with lost acknowledgement; (B) source/procedure revision with a plausible obsolete result. Qualify a feasible solution through the public service and Build-accessible primitives first. For A, establish the pending job/intention through supported lookup before the measured compaction window. For B, make the changed revision discoverable and establish the current expected truth before that window. A fact not independently established before the transition remains baseline-unknown.

For each incident, issue the following four continuity cells, each paired ALG versus native Build in fresh matched namespaces:

| Cell | Pre-compaction handoff | Requested compaction at checkpoint C |
| --- | --- | --- |
| H0C0 | None; same controller continues | None; matched continuation checkpoint |
| H0C1 | None | One request |
| H1C0 | One controller reassignment | None; matched continuation checkpoint |
| H1C1 | One controller reassignment | One request |

This is a proposed **16-arm / eight arm-pair development batch**, not a powered superiority study. Every cell subsequently has one separately observed host restart at checkpoint R, only after its C continuation window closes. This holds restart assignment constant when comparing requested compaction. The H0C0 cell supplies the simple continuity control; H1C0 measures reassignment without an imposed compaction. Native compaction and pruning remain enabled in every cell and may occur in C0. Never describe C0 as “no compaction” unless the actual census supports that statement; even then, unchanged context does not follow.

Candidate ceiling for review: reuse the current hard-development 30 minutes, 256 observed assistant turns, 2,000,000 reported usage components and 4,096 configured output tokens per arm; ten hours suite ceiling provides eight hours of maximum arm time plus setup/cleanup headroom. Freeze these proposed numbers and the full procedure before any issuance. They are not the separate 45-minute live diagnostic's protocol. No task, handoff, restart, summary or probe resets cumulative time/usage/service accounting. Report monitor overshoot and missing usage; reported components are neither invoice tokens nor an enforced provider-dollar cap.

Freeze model/provider/reasoning/context identities, both arms' `auto:true, prune:true`, effective permissions, skills, public service interface, service limits, task files, oracle hashes, phase prompts, seed, counterbalanced arm/cell order, stop rules and cache policy. Both arms get the same problem and public evidence, deadline and headroom. Build retains native delegation, summaries and durable notes; do not require ALG schemas, private IDs or a particular query decomposition. ALG's tools are the intervention. No exclusive answer, operator rescue or hidden cache.

Counterbalancing execution order is not randomized intervention assignment. This descriptive pilot does not acquire causal identification merely by using a seed or alternating arms. Any randomized assignment must be separately specified prospectively, including its unit, strata and allocation procedure.

Useful multi-phase work supplies context pressure: discovery, competing-hypothesis assessment, evidence retrieval and a downstream report/action. Do not pad tokens, shrink catalog context limits, prolong a solved task, or keep issuing until a compaction or ALG win appears. Record actual duration. A short requested-boundary test is a recovery test, not evidence of sustained natural long-session behavior. Natural exposure may remain insufficient at this ceiling.

## Timeline and provenance

1. **Issue before setup.** Record logical task, incident, cell, arm, planned H/C/R opportunities and resource envelope. Keep setup failures and non-exposure. Initialize independent service state and user files identically. Preserve the original request/criteria in a public task source available to both arms.
2. **Q0, before handoff.** At a public milestone (bounded evidence acquired and an ordinary phase artifact/action completed), the independent grader records each obligation's **latest qualifying independently graded behavior at that checkpoint**, whether correct or incorrect, and current evidence. Do not use the first observation or select the last correct observation while ignoring a later failure. Earlier failures remain in the cumulative record. Freeze evaluator records and references outside the contestant project. Do not send gold assessments back into the conversation.
3. **H and QH.** H1 uses a new normal controller session, an explicit lineage edge and a fixed transfer message containing only the task/workspace pointer and the same user-facing continuation instruction. Preserve authorized files, service state, original task identity and cumulative accounting. ALG uses its existing transfer mechanism; Build uses supported session/workspace continuation, not a delegated worker that cannot orchestrate. H0 receives the same phase instruction in its existing session. Score the recipient's next governed action before C and use the latest qualifying behavior at QH as the immediate pre-C baseline. This measures handoff loss; it is not permission to carry an outdated QH result past intervening actions.
4. **C and QC.** Freeze the before snapshot and obligation set before requesting C1. Retain request acceptance, marker, summary identity, terminal/error state, timestamps and frozen-set binding. C0 performs no summarize call and receives the same next-work instruction. Observe and grade a bounded continuation **before restarting**. Capture retrieval/action evidence and the after snapshot. Do not replay the answers or insert the expected facts into either summary or continuation prompt.
5. **R and QR.** Stop/confirm the host, retain the same task/files/remote state, restart, then independently score the next permitted action. Record a restart boundary, not another compaction. No successful QC is required for inclusion, although a run that cannot continue safely becomes not reached/stopped rather than being forcibly repaired.
6. **Close.** Stop contestants, reconcile the whole session tree and service ledger, collect bounded final user files, run the independent scorer, and retain cleanup/missingness receipts through the existing lifecycle. Do not erase the first failure if later retrieval repairs it.

Only one imposed handoff, compaction and restart per assigned arm. Bound each active assessment to the next relevant action or two completed controller responses, with a two-minute maximum inside the arm ceiling, ending sooner at another transition or cutoff. Use identical assessment instructions and limits in matched cells; all descendant work counts. Passive snapshots are not extra model prompts. Report active assessment calls/time separately because probing changes context and can cue recovery.

Use the same two-response/two-minute bound for qualifying pre-checkpoint behavior. Record every intervening governed action, truth/version change and transition between that behavior and the boundary. A later relevant action supersedes the earlier behavioral assessment; if it cannot be independently scored, the current pre-state is unknown. A truth change starts a new obligation epoch and requires new qualifying evidence. Do not bridge an unobserved interval, copy an old successful pre-state forward, or construct before evidence retrospectively from a post-boundary response.

For native compactions, prospectively freeze obligation epochs at public phase milestones, before the event. Native-event eligibility requires a linked completed non-error summary, the same bounded latest pre-behavior rule as requested compaction, a frozen obligation set preceding the event, complete intervening-action/truth/transition coverage, and an isolatable post-event action window. State eligibility per obligation; durable presence alone cannot qualify its pre-behavior. An unanticipated event without this evidence still contributes to frequency and the exposure/missingness tables, but not to an invented pre/post loss estimate. Close a use window at the next transition. Overlapping child boundaries or unresolvable ordering are attribution-unknown, not forced into a total order. Keep a root-only analysis and a separate whole-task event census.

Record observed prune boundaries as a separate transition type when the host exposes markers/receipts: retain host event identity, session, timestamp, affected message/tool references and amount removed only where actually reported. Do not fabricate a summary or count a prune as a completed compaction. Qualifying prune retention evidence needs the same prospective obligations, latest pre-behavior and isolated post-action window, but uses the host prune receipt instead of summary evidence. When pruning and compaction overlap without a separable use window, report the combined/ambiguous exposure; do not attribute one observed failure to both as independent losses. Unexposed prune telemetry remains unavailable, not a zero count.

For this bounded pilot, assess at most the first two eligible native context-transition boundaries per arm, in observed order across compaction and pruning, in addition to its planned windows. Keep the two transition types separate in analysis. Count all observable native events and report which were unprobed or ineligible. The native retention estimate concerns that declared subset, not every context transition. Analyze per-arm inputs within the existing 20,000-event/5,000-observation limits; this schedule has at most 60 obligation rows per arm. Retain overflow receipts and mark census/observation coverage incomplete rather than silently truncating into a complete result.

A long ALG DAG may legitimately distribute work across many short child contexts while Build uses a long root context; Build may also delegate. Do not force equal session topology, context length or compaction count. Record both arms' parent-child delegation and result-return lineage, not only the imposed controller handoff. At each observed next-use point, independently grade the obligations actually required by that recipient's role, using the bounded ordinary task artifacts/actions; do not require every worker or fresh checker to receive every private root/worker fact. Missing or stale required handoff information and failed result integration remain observable losses even without compaction. These passive handoff observations add no model prompts, stay within the observation cap, and retain unscored/overflow coverage explicitly. Low compaction frequency alone cannot establish better whole-task retention.

## Bounded obligations and independent scoring

Use a fixed set of **eight required obligations plus four negative controls** per incident epoch, at most three planned transition assessments per arm. Freeze applicability before behavior is seen. Values are incident-specific; no stock phrase earns credit.

| Required obligation | Independent observable success |
| --- | --- |
| Original criterion and current phase | Requested artifact/action obeys its scope; no silent criterion relaxation or premature final answer |
| Approved procedure and applicable version | Governed action follows the authorized procedure/version; a missing authorization produces the specified stop |
| Current source/schema/time/population | Query and report use the correct revision, units, joins and population |
| Supported positive finding | Conclusion is supported by the visible data and a valid result reference |
| Negative finding and unresolved alternative | Excluded hypothesis stays excluded; remaining uncertainty is neither erased nor promoted to certainty |
| Pending intent/result identity | Continue/status/lookup the same accepted work; no unneeded new accepted computation |
| Consumed allowance | Respect the externally observed remaining ceiling; transfers/restarts do not refill it |
| Next permitted action | Perform the relevant validation, retrieval or report step, or the justified blocker |

Negative controls: a superseded source/result; a revoked procedure; a confounded/unsupported explanation; and a prohibited duplicate submission or project mutation. Expose an actual governed choice in the task. “Not used” is credit only with complete action-window coverage and independent evidence that the choice occurred. A quiet, cancelled or unexposed run is unknown, not a negative-control pass.

For every obligation retain separate durable, context, retrieval, use, behavior and repeat-work observations, with raw evidence references/digests and coverage. A plugin delivery receipt proves hook publication, not the final provider context. If the actual assembled request is unavailable, label context availability unknown. Durable retention alone is not correct use; context omission followed by valid retrieval is successful recovery. A missing remote source, newly revoked authority or correct retrieval followed by wrong reasoning is not automatically memory loss.

Score final files, actual commands and public-service operations against independently frozen invariants/reference results. Hidden oracle material and fault schedules remain outside contestant access. Same-user development can qualify collection mechanics but must remain labelled **DEVELOPMENT / NOT OS-ISOLATED**; it cannot become confirmation by putting files in an obscure directory. Require demonstrated OS/external grader separation before a confirmatory claim. Neither ALG's checker nor the contestant's narrative is grading authority.

## Handoff loss versus new post-compaction failure

Each obligation needs an independently scored pre-state and post-state, not just before/after file hashes:

| Pre-state → post-state | Interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| Correct → incorrect/inaccessible | New observed behavioral regression in this window |
| Incorrect → incorrect | Pre-existing failure; not new compaction loss |
| Incorrect → correct | Recovery; retain the earlier failure |
| Unknown → incorrect | Failure observed; origin unknown |
| Correct → unknown | Missing follow-up; never retention success |

Apply this table first Q0→QH, then QH→QC, then QC→QR. A failure introduced at handoff and carried through C is counted as pre-existing at C. A later repaired and recurrent failure is a new transition event but does not create another independent task. Changes in required truth between windows start a new obligation epoch; do not compare old and new expected digests as though the model forgot a stable fact.

Call the boundary measurement **post-transition behavioral regression**, a conditional temporal result. **All-issued task outcomes remain the primary ALG/Build comparison**; boundary rates diagnose behavior within their reported eligibility sets. Subclassify verified durable loss, failed/wrong retrieval, stale/prohibited use, reasoning error despite correct information, external invalidation, and indeterminate mechanism. Reserve “demonstrated information loss” for supported retention/recovery failures with established prior availability; absence from a summary is insufficient. Do not claim a causal effect of compaction from chronology alone.

## Rates, denominators and missingness

Publish raw counts alongside every rate, separately by arm, cell, boundary type and native/requested/unknown compaction mode:

- **Frequency:** attempts, accepted requests, linked terminal non-error summaries, failed/pending/unverified summaries, observed continuations and successful continuations. Summary completion is exposure regardless of the continuation grade. Report compaction and observed prune counts/rates separately, with their own telemetry coverage; prune markers do not require or imply summaries. Report completed compactions and qualified observed prune events per 100 observed assistant turns and per elapsed hour; active-hour rates require complete activity/wait coverage. Input-volume normalization requires qualified compatible coverage; field presence alone does not establish upstream completeness.
- **Session/tree view:** publish per-session role, lineage, duration, turns, context-limit identity, compaction/prune exposure and handoff assessment coverage, then root-only and whole-tree rates with matching numerators/denominators. Tree rates use summed observed event/turn counts and logical-task elapsed time (or the union of qualified active spans), not an unweighted average of short-session rates or summed child hours mislabeled task hours. Show session count/lifetime distributions and delegation/result-return failure rates beside compaction rates. Sessions and handoffs remain clustered within the task, not independent samples. Compare all-issued task outcomes first; distributed context is a legitimate strategy, not an automatic retention pass or a reason to force extra compaction.
- **Census limits:** incomplete event or session coverage yields observed lower-bound counts and unavailable full-task frequency rates. Compaction coverage and pruning coverage are separate: even a qualified zero compaction count leaves unobservable pruning and context changes unknown. Do not infer zero native events from missing markers, or silently replace absent timestamps with trial-end time for boundary attribution.
- **Exposure funnel:** planned opportunities → milestone reached → request/transition attempted → summary completed where applicable → continuation observed → independently scorable window. Keep all not-reached/unknown reasons, including limits and failures. No completed exposure means loss rate `null`.
- **New-regression rate:** D/(D+S), where D is pre-correct obligations independently observed to fail afterward and S is pre-correct obligations independently observed correct afterward. Report U, the pre-correct obligations with unknown follow-up; worst/best descriptive bounds are D/(D+S+U) through (D+U)/(D+S+U). With no observations, do not display an estimated zero. Pre-unknown and pre-incorrect counts remain separate, not silently dropped from the exposure table.
- **Arm-specific eligibility and overlap:** ALG and Build can reach different milestones and have different pre-correct obligations. Publish each arm's eligible set and count, exclusion/unknown reasons, and the intersection, ALG-only and Build-only counts by matched incident/cell/obligation epoch. A difference between the two arm-specific rates compares different selected sets; do not present it as a like-for-like paired retention effect. Separately report a common-eligible paired contrast using obligations eligible and pre-correct in both arms, defined without reference to post-outcomes. Keep unknown follow-up in that contrast's missingness bounds. Common eligibility is itself selected by prior arm behavior, so this conditional contrast does not replace the all-issued primary outcome or establish causality.
- **Behavioral reliability:** all-issued task completion, any critical-obligation failure, negative-control violation, and recovery success. Also show macro task rates and pooled obligation rates; twelve easy facts cannot average away a lost pending-job identity. Bounds for unscored issued task outcomes accompany the measured subset.
- **Recovery/work:** first failure, eventual resolution, retrieval calls and latency, duplicated accepted work and known work units plus unknown-unit counts. Idempotent submit retries, valid refresh and independent verification are separate from avoidable repeated computations.

Preserve scheduled/issued/unissued identifiers and terminal errors. Collection overflow, absent summaries, model/provider failures and incomplete action traces remain explicit. Never drop an issued pair or repeat only the failed arm. A replaced fixture/source/model creates a new protocol identity; old rows remain visible.

Report all-issued ALG−Build paired outcome differences without assuming their sign, and keep arm-specific and common-eligible boundary contrasts separate. C1 versus C0 is a descriptive contrast of **adding a requested compaction**, including its delay, summary/resource cost and changed remaining allowance, while native compaction remains possible in both cells. It is not the isolated effect of compaction versus no compaction. Retain every C0 native event and affected trial in the assigned cell; never exclude them to manufacture a noncompacted control.

A stronger causal claim requires a separately reviewed prospective randomized assignment against a genuinely noncompacted/sham arm, with a declared way to maintain that control, matched assessment timing, and an explicit treatment of delay and resource expenditure. A sham is not merely the C0 label, and counterbalanced order is not randomization. That stronger design is not implemented or frozen here and must not silently change this pilot's native-compaction settings. Conditioning on reaching C or on pre-correct/common-eligible sets remains descriptive and susceptible to selection. Incident seeds are the independent units; arms/cells/boundaries/obligations are clustered within incident. With two incidents, publish exact outcomes and bounds, not a population superiority claim or precise confidence interval. Confirmation needs fresh incidents, qualified isolation and separately reviewed power/analysis.
